Starting Guide
Cell generation works in a way reminiscent of mitosis. You start with a certain number of cells (starting cells), and your Karyofox iterates through multiple generations. Each time the progress bar is filled, your cells advance a generation, and each of your cells has a chance of multiplying. At larger numbers of cells in holding, the mitosis chance value essentially becomes the value that your cells in holding are multiplied by after a generation. After your cells have advanced the maximum number of generations, the number of cells in holding is added to your total cells. This value is known as cell output.
